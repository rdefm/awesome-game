# 19 — Night on Bluebell

**What to build:** A moonflower in the meadow. Tap it and dusk falls across Bluebell: the sky deepens, stars come out, fireflies drift over the meadow, the bluebells glow faintly and the grove's mushrooms and spores glow brighter. Tap it again and the sun comes up. The pod's window shows the same time of day.
