# 09 — Bed nook in the pod

**What to build:** A round, cushioned bed nook tucked into the pod wall with a little curtain. Tap it and she climbs in and snoozes (Zzz), the pod dimming and its string lights glowing softer, until she's tapped. Drop a friend in and it's tucked in to nap; picking it up wakes it.
