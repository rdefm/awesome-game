# 10 — Pantry cupboard and kettle in the pod

**What to build:** A little cupboard and a kettle on a stove in the pod. Tap the cupboard and its doors swing open on jars: a nectar pot or a seed cookie pops out (two new snacks, ready-made — no cooking). Tap the kettle and it heats up, rattles and whistles a cheerful tune with a puff of steam.
