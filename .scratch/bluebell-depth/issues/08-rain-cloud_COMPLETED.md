# 08 — Rain cloud and rainbow

**What to build:** A fluffy little cloud low over the meadow. Tap it and it rains on that patch: the bluebells under it ring faster, and a puddle forms that she splashes in when she walks through it. When the rain stops a rainbow arcs over the meadow for a while, and the puddle dries up.
