# 15 — Fairy ring in the grove

**What to build:** A ring of tiny mushrooms on the grove floor. Tap it and she steps inside: a twinkle, and she shrinks to tiny, so everything around looks giant and her steps squeak. Tap the ring again (or anywhere outside it) and she hops out and grows back with a pop. Friends dropped in shrink too.
