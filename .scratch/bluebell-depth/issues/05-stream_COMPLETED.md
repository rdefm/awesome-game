# 05 — Stream and stepping stones

**What to build:** A little stream across the far side of the meadow. Stepping stones across it ring a rising note as she hops each one. Drop a thing in the water and it bobs off downstream, out of sight, then washes up on the bank a moment later; drop a friend in and it paddles across and shakes itself dry.
