# 05 — Sound effects

**What to build:** Short sound cues for pick up, drop/place, tap, and goal-satisfied, played through Phaser's sound manager. No background music. Source of the actual SFX files (free pack vs. generated) is an open decision from the spec — pick a reasonable free source (e.g. Kenney.nl audio packs) unless the user directs otherwise.

**Blocked by:** 02, 04

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A pick-up sound plays when an entity is picked up (tap or drag path)
- [ ] A drop/place sound plays when an entity is placed
- [ ] A tap sound plays on tap interactions distinct from pick-up/place where applicable
- [ ] A distinct "goal satisfied" sound plays when a character's wanted item is successfully given
- [ ] Sound files are sourced and credited/licensed appropriately for redistribution in the project
